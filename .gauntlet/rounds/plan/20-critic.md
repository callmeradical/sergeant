# Plan critic — round 20

## Winner

Quality bar. The backlog half was defined away.

## Largest gap

Only 54 of 492 non-closed cards belonged transitively to named phases. The plan
allowed every other card to be deferred, while no later phase implemented
deferred work. Every gate could pass with 89% of the backlog untouched.

## Exact challenge

Map every card to an implementation wave or verified closure. Make deferral
individual and human-approved with owner/trigger; add a phase that executes all
remaining waves; require zero P0/P1 and zero unresolved in-progress/in-review at
final closure.

# Plan critic — round 17

## Winner

Quality bar. The secret gate would destroy the only shipping path.

## Largest gap

Origin embedded a credential, while no SSH remote or credential helper existed.
The plan required revocation without naming replacement auth, yet mandated a
fetch through the exposed URL before running the gate.

## Exact challenge

Configure a credential-free remote plus external auth, prove read and dry-run
write, revoke the exposed credential, then prove both again. Own a secret-safe
remote checker in an earlier phase and prohibit fetch until it passes.
